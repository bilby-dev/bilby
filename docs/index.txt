=====
bilby
=====

**A user-friendly Bayesian inference library.**

Bilby provides a simple interface to perform parameter estimation with a wide
range of stochastic samplers. It is primarily designed and built for inference
of compact binary coalescence events in interferometric data, but it can also
be used for more general problems.
